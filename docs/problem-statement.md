# ?? KPIT Sparkle 2027: Problem Statement & Agronomic Challenges

## 1. Challenge Overview & Scope

**KPIT Sparkle 2027** invites engineering and scientific innovations addressing sustainable national and global infrastructure challenges. Under the Smart Agriculture domain, this project addresses:

> **AI for Crop Monitoring, Moisture Stress Detection & Irrigation Advisory**

Agriculture accounts for over **70% of freshwater withdrawals globally** and employs a vast portion of the workforce in developing nations like India. However, contemporary farming operations are severely vulnerable to erratic monsoon patterns, climate variability, inefficient groundwater depletion, and suboptimal crop selection.

## 2. Core Operational Challenges

### A. The Crop Selection & Suitability Dilemma
Farmers frequently select crops based on historical habit or immediate market speculation rather than rigorous agro-ecological compatibility. When crops are planted in soil with suboptimal pH, deficient micronutrients, or unfavorable seasonal climate envelopes, productivity drops drastically, and susceptibility to pests and diseases surges.

### B. Moisture Stress & Water Deficit
Water stress in crops manifests in two destructive extremes:
1. **Prolonged Moisture Deficit (Drought Stress)**: Leads to stomatal closure, impaired photosynthesis, stunted vegetative biomass, flower dropping, and permanent wilting.
2. **Excessive Soil Saturation (Waterlogging)**: Causes root hypoxia, nutrient leaching, fungal proliferation, and soil salinization.

Crucially, **invisible physiological stress** occurs days before visible wilting appears on canopy foliage. Conventional manual inspection detects water stress only after significant yield loss has already become irreversible.

### C. The Irrigation Decision Problem
Irrigation scheduling in standard practice is predominantly reactive or timer-based (heuristic):
* **Over-irrigation**: Leaches critical nitrates and potassium past the rootzone, wastes precious electrical energy, and depletes critical aquifer reserves.
* **Under-irrigation**: Induces cumulative water stress during sensitive phenological stages (e.g., flowering, grain filling).
* **Missing Context**: Typical irrigation decisions fail to integrate real-time atmospheric evaporative demand ($ET_0$), multi-depth soil texture infiltration rates, 7-day precipitation probabilities, and terrain-driven runoff risks.

## 3. Objective of GeoAgri-AI

GeoAgri-AI is formulated to transition agricultural decision-making from subjective intuition to an **evidence-based, geospatial-first, multi-objective decision-support framework**. 

By transforming multi-spectral spatial signals, multi-depth soil chemistry, real-time meteorological observations, and physiological crop knowledge into actionable advisories, GeoAgri-AI empowers farmers and agronomists to:
1. Identify the most resilient and productive crops tailored to exact local micro-climates.
2. Estimate and forecast crop water stress prior to visible canopy degradation.
3. Optimize irrigation timing, volume, and scheduling to conserve water while maximizing harvest yields.
